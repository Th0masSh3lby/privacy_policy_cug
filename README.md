# Privacy Policy — Can U Gamble?

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Can U Gamble? ("the game") is developed by Aravind. This policy explains what
information the game handles.

## Information we collect
The current version of the game **does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal
information**. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

## Information stored on your device
Your game progress (bankroll, perks, settings and statistics) is saved only on your device, in the
game's private storage. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

## Children
The game is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

## Real money
The game does not offer real-money gambling, purchases of in-game currency, or prizes of any kind.

## Changes
If a future version adds advertising or in-app purchases, this policy will be updated before that
version is released, and will describe the data those services process.
